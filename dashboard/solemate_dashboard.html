How we analyze your health ›
Solemate watches three signals. Motion from the accelerometer detects falls and counts steps. Temperature compares the two feet; a sustained difference above about 2 °C can be an early sign of inflammation. Pressure shows how weight is spread across the foot, flagging uneven loading. This prototype shows one foot, so the health status here is a simplified demonstration based on pressure balance; the production device uses left-vs-right temperature asymmetry.
